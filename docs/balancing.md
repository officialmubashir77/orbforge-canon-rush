# Balance and regression plan

- Confirm a new user can complete Level 1 with the starting cannon and no upgrades.
- Measure Level 1–3 duration, average player damage, hit percentage, number of shots and coin accumulation. Tune early encounters toward a short session.
- Verify the 85-coin Rapid Blaster unlock is attainable through regular play; avoid upgrades being mandatory for levels 1–3.
- Compare 60-second simulated DPS across Classic, Rapid and Tri-Shot against stationary vs moving balls.
- Check projectile pool saturation at 15 shots/s + three streams and cap emitted objects if needed.
- Validate wall/ground bounce normals and no stuck orbs on multiple aspect ratios.
- Test save corruption, app backgrounding during play, upgrade purchase and interrupted save rotation.
- Establish actual Android frame-time metrics (60 FPS target, not guaranteed) on multiple mid-range devices.
